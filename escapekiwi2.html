PORTFOLIO

Kiana Rodriguez is a 24-year old queer Dominican-Puertorican-American who
takes pride in creating illustrations and comics.


Coming from humble beginnings with both parents having managed to escape
 poverty, Kiana started drawing at early as 3, the creative talent being passed down
from her father, who studied to be an architect and worked in engineering.


Kiana's art apparoach shifted after the passing of her father, where her hobby
 now had purpose. She started creating violent, erotic & melancholic drawings
through her emotions and grievances, with a lot of her work being jarring and
dramatic but not unnoticed, sketching and creating her own little worlds for years.


In spite of mental health struggles and lack of motivation to continue her craft,
 Rodriguez pushed through by completing college and finding her passions, along
with the style she works best in.
